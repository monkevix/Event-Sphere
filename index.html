Have Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can register on EventSphere?

Any currently enrolled student with a valid college email ID can create an account and register for events.

Is there a fee to use EventSphere?

Creating an account and browsing events is completely free. Some events may have their own participation fee, shown on the event's details page.

Can I register for multiple events?

Yes, you can register for as many events as you like, as long as their schedules don't clash.

How do I get updates about an event?

Once registered, updates are shared via your registered college email and shown on the event's details page.
