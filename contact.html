Often asked

How fast do you reply?

Under 12 hours on weekdays, under 24 hours on weekends. For urgent event-day issues, call the helpline.

Can I get a refund for a paid event?

Refunds follow the official department's policy for that event. Contact us and we’ll connect you to the concerned department.

Can I attend events from other departments?

Yes — if your eligibility matches the event (usually open to all Chitkara students). Check the event details page for “Who can join”.
