Our Story

Built by students, for students.

We were tired of missing events because info was scattered across posters, WhatsApp groups and last-minute announcements. So we built EventSphere — one clean place for every campus event.

Our Mission

Make campus life discoverable. Whether it’s a hackathon at midnight or a cultural fest that takes over the grounds — no student should hear about it after it’s over.

EventSphere started as a final-year FEE project for Chitkara University. We handle discovery, registration, and reminders so students can focus on showing up — everything listed here is curated by the university.

What we value

Principles we build on

Student-first

Every decision starts with: does this help a student discover or join an event faster?

Inclusive by default

Accessible design, vernacular-friendly copy, low-bandwidth friendly pages.

Always up to date

Journey

How we got here

2024 — The Poster Problem

Idea born in a corridor

Two friends missed a hackathon because the poster was behind a pillar. They sketched EventSphere on a napkin that night.

2025 — First Campus Pilot

One college, 18 events

Launched with the Cultural Committee. 600 sign-ups in week one. Zero queues at registration desks.

2026 — Live at Chitkara

Growing across campus

Now covering tech fests, sports leagues, workshops and competitions across every department at Chitkara University.
